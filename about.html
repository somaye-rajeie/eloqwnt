Who we are

Eloqwnt is a global creative studio built for tech and corporate leaders navigating pivotal moments — pre-raise, post-acquisition, expansion, or full repositioning.

Linur Chubaev (CEO, Founder)
Mirhayot Yunusov (Design Director, co-Founder)

Design-first. Built for business outcomes.

Eloqwnt is a strategic design partner for growth-stage tech and capital-driven companies that need stronger credibility, sharper positioning, and a market-facing system built for growth.

We do not treat design as decoration or one-off execution. We diagnose what is slowing trust, then turn brand, web, and product into assets that support fundraising, conversion, and expansion.
